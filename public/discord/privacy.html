Jev Yarn for Discord

Privacy Policy

Last updated September 22, 2026

Jev Yarn in Discord has no accounts, no ads and no analytics. It keeps what a game needs only while you play, and it never sells your information or shares it for advertising. "We" means the developer of Jev Yarn.

What the game handles

  • What you enter: your nickname, the icon you pick, themes, the lines you write, the line you tap as your favorite, and, if you set the theme, the judging sliders.
  • Game details: your score, when you joined, whether you are connected or have the game in the background, whether you wrote in the last round, and a random token that puts you back in your seat if the game reloads.
  • Connection details: your IP address, used while you are connected to limit how many connections one network can open to a game.
  • From Discord: an ID for the Activity session, which keeps your call's game separate from every other call's. The game does not ask for your Discord account, so it never receives your Discord username, ID or friends list.

On your device

The game saves your nickname, icon and seat token in the storage Discord gives it, so you can rejoin. The game sets no cookies.

How it is used

Only to run the game you chose to play: to show your nickname, icon, lines and score to the players in your call's game, to keep each call's game separate, to have Jev judge lines and score stories, to filter content, and to stop abuse. Players' favorite picks adjust the game's default judging sliders while the call's game lasts.

Who else receives it

  • Other players in your call's game see your nickname, icon, score and everything you write, and any of them can copy, download or share a finished story.
  • TypeSafe runs Jev. It receives each round's lines, the theme and the story so far, to pick the winner and filter lines; each finished story, to score it; and nicknames and themes, to check them. Lines are sent without players' names. TypeSafe says it does not train or fine-tune AI models on this input. Its privacy policy applies to what it receives.
  • Cloudflare hosts the game and stores each call's game. It keeps technical logs of requests and errors for 7 days. Its privacy policy applies.
  • Discord runs the Activity and relays the game. Its privacy policy applies to your use of Discord.

We share nothing with anyone else unless the law requires it. These companies may process information in the United States and other countries where they operate. The game is always served over encrypted connections.

How long it is kept

  • Your seat, with your nickname, icon, score and token, is deleted when you leave, or about a minute after your connection drops.
  • A story in progress is deleted when the last player leaves the game.
  • Each call's game is deleted entirely ten minutes after everyone has left it.
  • Cloudflare's logs are kept for 7 days. TypeSafe keeps data as its privacy policy describes.
  • What the game saved in Discord's storage stays until Discord clears it.

Children

Jev Yarn in Discord is for ages 13 and up. It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Your choices

Leave the game to delete your seat. Because there are no accounts and seats are deleted when you leave, we generally hold nothing we could connect to you afterwards.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the current version.

Contact

Questions or requests: open an issue at github.com/cedrecs/jev-yarn/issues. Issues are public, so do not post personal information there.
