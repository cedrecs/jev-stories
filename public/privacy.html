Jev Yarn

Privacy Policy

Last updated September 22, 2026

Jev Yarn has no accounts, no ads and no analytics. It keeps what a game needs only while you play, and it never sells your information or shares it for advertising. "We" means the developer of Jev Yarn. This policy covers Jev Yarn on the web; Jev Yarn in Discord has its own privacy policy.

What the game handles

  • What you enter: your nickname, the icon you pick, themes, the lines you write, the line you tap as your favorite, and, if you set the theme, the judging sliders.
  • Game details: your score, when you joined, whether you are connected or have the game in the background, whether you wrote in the last round, and a random token that puts you back in your seat after a refresh.
  • Connection details: your IP address, used while you are connected to limit how many connections one network can open to a room.

On your device

The game saves your nickname, icon and seat tokens in your browser's storage so you can rejoin, and your browser may keep a copy of the game's files so it opens faster. The game sets no cookies.

How it is used

Only to run the game you chose to play: to show your nickname, icon, lines and score to the players in your room, to keep rooms apart, to have Jev judge lines and score stories, to filter content in the filtered rooms, and to stop abuse. Players' favorite picks slowly adjust each room's default judging sliders; those defaults are shared by the whole room and do not identify anyone.

Who else receives it

  • Other players in your room see your nickname, icon, score and everything you write, and any of them can copy, download or share a finished story.
  • TypeSafe runs Jev. It receives each round's lines, the theme and the story so far, to pick the winner and filter lines; each finished story, to score it; and, in Safe for Everyone, Moderated for Teens and Mature Audience Only, nicknames and themes, to check them. Lines are sent without players' names. TypeSafe says it does not train or fine-tune AI models on this input. Its privacy policy applies to what it receives.
  • Cloudflare hosts the game and stores its rooms. It keeps technical logs of requests and errors for 7 days. Its privacy policy applies.

We share nothing with anyone else unless the law requires it. These companies may process information in the United States and other countries where they operate. The game is always served over encrypted connections.

How long it is kept

  • Your seat, with your nickname, icon, score and token, is deleted when you leave, or about a minute after your connection drops.
  • A story in progress is deleted when the last player leaves its room.
  • A room clears everything else after an hour with nobody in it. Only its default judging sliders remain, and they contain no personal information.
  • Cloudflare's logs are kept for 7 days. TypeSafe keeps data as its privacy policy describes.
  • What the game saved in your browser stays until you clear the site's data.

Children

Jev Yarn is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. Mature Audience Only and Absolute Degenerates are for adults 18 and over.

Your choices

Leave a room to delete your seat, and clear your browser's data for the game to remove what it saved on your device. Because there are no accounts and seats are deleted when you leave, we generally hold nothing we could connect to you afterwards.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the current version.

Contact

Questions or requests: open an issue at github.com/cedrecs/jev-yarn/issues. Issues are public, so do not post personal information there.
